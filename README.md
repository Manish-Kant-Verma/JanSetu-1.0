# 🇮🇳 JanSetu

## Connecting Citizens, Government for Faster Civic Action

**JanSetu** is a citizen-centric civic problem reporting and government assistance platform designed to create a direct, transparent, evidence-based connection between **citizens, verified student volunteers, and government departments**.

The platform enables citizens to report local civic problems, allows other verified citizens from the affected locality to confirm those problems, consolidates repeated reports, and forwards verified cases to the appropriate government authority.

At the same time, JanSetu creates a network of selected students from colleges and technical institutes who can assist government departments with **field assessment, documentation, data collection, and report preparation** when such assistance is requested.

> **Citizens identify → Community verifies → JanSetu consolidates → Government acts → Students assist when requested → Citizens track**

---

# 🎯 Vision

JanSetu aims to build a digital civic ecosystem where local problems can be identified and documented efficiently, communities can contribute evidence, government departments receive structured information, and qualified students can provide additional technical and field-level assistance.

The platform is designed around three principles:

* **Citizen participation**
* **Evidence-based reporting**
* **Direct government coordination**

JanSetu does not replace government authorities or their decision-making processes. It provides them with better-organized information and optional assistance from a trained volunteer network.

---

# 🚨 The Problem

Civic problems such as damaged roads, broken street lights, garbage accumulation, drainage issues, water-supply problems, and damaged public infrastructure are common in many localities.

However, several challenges can occur:

* Multiple citizens report the same problem separately.
* Some complaints lack sufficient evidence.
* Authorities may receive incomplete information.
* Citizens may not know the current status of a complaint.
* Preliminary field assessment can require additional time.
* Local technical information may not be immediately available.
* There can be a gap between identifying a problem and providing useful documentation to the responsible department.

JanSetu attempts to address these challenges through **community verification, evidence collection, complaint consolidation, and an optional student volunteer network**.

---

# 💡 The JanSetu Solution

JanSetu consists of two connected systems.

### 1. Citizen Civic Reporting System

Citizens can:

* Report civic problems
* Upload photographs
* Provide location information
* Describe the problem
* Select the relevant category
* Verify problems reported by other citizens from their locality
* Track the status of cases

Repeated reports concerning the same issue can be consolidated into a single civic case.

### 2. Student Civic Volunteer Network

JanSetu can connect participating colleges and technical institutes to create a pool of **selected and trained student volunteers**.

Government departments or authorized officers can request assistance from this network when they require:

* Field assessment
* Documentation
* Data collection
* Preliminary analysis
* Local situation reports
* Technical observations
* Other authorized reporting assistance

The two systems work together but remain functionally distinct.

# 🪪 Citizen Identity Verification

To improve authenticity and reduce abuse, JanSetu can use an identity-verification process.

Depending on the final implementation and applicable legal requirements, verification may involve:

* Government-issued identity
* Aadhaar or another legally appropriate identity mechanism
* Mobile verification
* Locality/address verification

### Privacy principle

Sensitive identity information should **never be publicly exposed**.

Identity verification exists to establish that an account represents a genuine participant and to support locality-based verification.

---

# 📍 Locality-Based Verification

One of the core concepts of JanSetu is that **verified citizens from the affected locality can confirm whether a reported problem actually exists**.

### Example

A citizen reports:

> "A large pothole has developed near a particular road."

Other verified citizens living in the relevant locality can view the report and confirm the problem.

```text
Citizen A
   │
   │ Reports pothole
   ▼
JanSetu
   ▲
   │
Citizen B ── Confirms
Citizen C ── Confirms
Citizen D ── Confirms
Citizen E ── Confirms
```

This provides multiple independent observations of the same civic problem.

# 🔗 Duplicate Complaint Consolidation

A major problem in conventional complaint systems is that many citizens may submit separate complaints about the **same physical problem**.

For example:

```text
Report 01 ─┐
Report 02 ─┤
Report 03 ─┤
Report 04 ─┤
Report 05 ─┤
Report 06 ─┤
            ├──► CONSOLIDATED CIVIC CASE
Report 07 ─┤
Report 08 ─┤
Report 09 ─┤
Report 10 ─┘
```

JanSetu can identify potentially related reports using information such as:

* Geographic proximity
* Problem category
* Description similarity
* Photographic evidence
* Time of reporting
* Existing case information

The system can then associate related reports with a consolidated case.

This reduces unnecessary duplication while preserving supporting evidence from multiple citizens.

# 🎓 Student Civic Volunteer Network

JanSetu introduces a separate **Student Civic Volunteer Network**.

The goal is to connect colleges and technical institutes with civic-assistance opportunities.

Participating institutions can provide students who are interested in contributing to civic activities.

However, **not every interested student automatically becomes a JanSetu volunteer**.

Students go through a structured selection and evaluation process.

* Engineering colleges

The platform maintains a pool of selected volunteers based on their qualifications, skills, evaluation results, locality, and availability.

# 📝 Practical Report Assignment

After the initial screening, students can be given a practical assessment task.

They may be asked to create a structured report about a civic problem.

The report could include:

* Problem identification
* Location
* Detailed description
* Photographic evidence
* Measurements where appropriate
* Current condition
* Possible causes
* Impact
* Technical observations
* Required intervention
* Supporting information
* Other relevant observations

The purpose is to determine how accurately and effectively a student can **observe, analyze, document, and communicate a real-world civic problem**.

---

# 🧠 Report Accuracy Evaluation

A key part of the student selection process is practical accuracy.

JanSetu can maintain a collection of **previously assessed and resolved civic cases** whose reports have already been validated.

A student may be assigned a suitable assessment based on one of these previously resolved situations.

The student creates a report independently.

The system can then compare the student's report against the **validated reference information**.

```text
                 Previously Validated Case
                           │
                           ▼
                   Reference Report
                           │
                           │
                           ▼
Student ──► Field / Practical Assessment
                           │
                           ▼
                    Student Report
                           │
                           ▼
                 Accuracy Comparison
                           │
            ┌──────────────┼──────────────┐
            ▼              ▼              ▼
       Observations     Evidence       Details
            │              │              │
            └──────────────┼──────────────┘
                           ▼
                    Evaluation Result
```

Evaluation can consider:

* Correctness of observations
* Completeness
* Evidence quality
* Problem identification
* Location accuracy
* Technical understanding
* Consistency with validated information
* Quality of analysis
* Report structure

Students demonstrating strong reporting accuracy can be shortlisted for the volunteer network.

### Privacy Protection

Reference reports must be handled securely.

Students should not receive confidential citizen information or sensitive details from previous cases merely for the purpose of evaluation.

# 🚫 Volunteer Limitations

Student volunteers are **not government officials**.

They cannot:

* Issue government orders
* Approve government projects
* Make official departmental decisions
* Close government cases
* Override government officers
* Represent themselves as government officials
* Access restricted information without authorization
* Exercise governmental authority

Their role is limited to the **authorized assignment** provided through JanSetu.
